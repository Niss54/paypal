# KinetixRAG demo entry point

For the PayPal AI Hackathon submission, use the [under-three-minute recording script](JUDGE_VIDEO_SCRIPT_3_MIN.md) and [judge evaluation guide](../JUDGE_README.md).

The demonstration should establish three things with visible evidence:

1. The AI investigation can produce an answer grounded in retrieved material.
2. The application rejects a premature capture request at the server.
3. Evidence validation and a separate supervisor approval precede the application's successful Sandbox capture request.

The pump procurement scenario and supplier quotes are fixtures. PayPal Sandbox and simulation are separate modes. Submitted reports are not independently authenticated sensor evidence. GraphSAGE and ERP connectors are outside this recording's core claim.

For local setup and dependency requirements, see the [README](../README.md). For exact claims and their source-code evidence, see the [claim matrix](CLAIM_EVIDENCE_MATRIX.md).
